I'm a final-year PhD student in the School of Informatics at the University of Edinburgh, where I work on 3D computer vision and multi-modal machine learning. I collaborate with Dr. Hakan Bilen in the Visual Computing Group (VICO) and Dr. Changjian Li from the GraphVix lab.

I previously interned at Autodesk Research, developing VLM agents and working on agent optimization. Before that, I was a full-time research assistant at MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi, working on computer vision and machine learning with Dr. Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Dr. Salman Khan, and Dr. Hisham Cholakkal.

I earned my bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from Jadavpur University in Kolkata. As an undergraduate, I also worked with Dr. Yang Wang at the University of Manitoba, Canada, and Dr. Partha Roy at IIT Roorkee, India.
